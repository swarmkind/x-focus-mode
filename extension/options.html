Before you switch it on

Your AI provider receives eligible post text and your interests. Local mode sends them to Ollama on this computer. Community-only mode makes no AI requests.

The relay and peers receive tweet IDs, category codes, human/model source and model name, expiry, revision and your pseudonymous public key and signature. The key links your contributions. No tweet text, handles, interests, reveal history or API keys are shared with the community network.

Peer connections expose your public IP and requested tweet IDs to other peers. The relay can observe requests and IPs; infrastructure security logs may exist. Distributed reports can be copied and cannot be recalled. A signed withdrawal can supersede a report, but old copies may remain.

Both network settings start checked. Nothing activates until you accept below and save. You may turn either off independently at any time.
